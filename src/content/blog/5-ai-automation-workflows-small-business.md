# 5 AI Automation Workflows Every Small Business Should Implement

Small businesses wear many hats. Here are the automation workflows that deliver the biggest impact:

## 1. Lead Response Automation

Instantly respond to new leads with personalized AI-generated messages. No more waiting hours to follow up.

## 2. Invoice Processing

Automatically extract data from invoices, match with purchase orders, and route for approval.

## 3. Social Media Content Calendar

Generate weeks of social media posts in minutes using AI. Schedule and publish automatically.

## 4. Customer Support Triage

AI reads incoming support tickets, categorizes them, and drafts responses for your team to review.

## 5. Weekly Business Reports

Automatically compile sales, marketing, and operations data into a weekly report delivered every Monday.
